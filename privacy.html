Privacy

Idle Dominion

← Idle Dominion

Idle Dominion collects nothing about you.

That is the whole policy. The rest of this page is only the detail, so that the claim can be checked rather than taken on trust.

No data is collected

The app has no account system and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no attribution or measurement software, and no third-party software development kits of any kind. It does not ask for your location, your contacts, your camera, your microphone, your photos, or permission to track you across other apps and websites.

The app does not connect to the internet

Idle Dominion contains no networking code. It does not have a server, and there is nowhere for anything to be sent even in principle. The one exception is Apple's own App Store, which the app talks to when you make a purchase — see below.

What is stored, and where

Everything the game remembers is written to your device's own storage and stays there:

  • Your campaign. Which territories you hold, what you have built in each of them, what is in your treasury, and which deeds you have earned.
  • How long each castle took you. The game records the number of seconds each territory took to capture so that it can show its own difficulty curve. It is a design instrument, it is never transmitted, and it describes the game rather than you.
  • Your settings. Whether sound and haptics are on.

None of it leaves the device, and all of it is removed when you delete the app. There is no backup on our side to delete, because there is no our side. If your device backs itself up to iCloud, that backup is between you and Apple and is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one.

Purchases

Idle Dominion offers three optional purchases of in-game gold. These are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. The app is told only whether a purchase completed — it never sees, receives, or stores your name, your payment details, or your Apple Account. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Children

Because nothing is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children. The app has no chat, no user-generated content, no social features, and no links out to the web from within the game.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever collects anything, this page will say so before that version ships, and the change will be described in the app's release notes rather than only here.
